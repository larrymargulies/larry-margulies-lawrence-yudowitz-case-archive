Larry Margulies / Lawrence Yudowitz — Public Investigative Case Records Archive

A neutral, evidence-first repository of source materials organized for genuine review by citizens and, where relevant, law-enforcement personnel, professional investigators, attorneys, journalists, researchers and oversight bodies.

Important evidentiary notice. Complaints remain complaints. Allegations remain allegations unless independently established. Witness statements remain attributed statements, affidavits remain affidavits, and official records retain their actual provenance. Publication does not establish guilt, prove a claim, constitute a judicial finding or represent endorsement by any agency.

This archive preserves 15 original source records. Original historical documents and filenames are retained unchanged for provenance. Documented facts, attributed statements, allegations, inferences and unresolved information must remain clearly distinguished.

Review standard

Documented fact is directly established by an identified source record. An attributed statement identifies its speaker or document. An allegation is not treated as an established fact. An inference or analytical note requires verification. Unresolved or missing information identifies evidentiary gaps.
