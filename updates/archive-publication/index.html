Larry Margulies aka Lawrence Yudowitz Publishes 16-Document Case Archive

A public preservation archive now brings together 16 original files previously released through the Newsroom. The collection includes complaint-affidavits, exhibits, supplemental filings, correspondence and related source records.

The materials concern Larry Margulies, also known as Lawrence Yudowitz, and a complaint charge of solicitation to commit murder, threats, cross-jurisdictional activity and institutional responses.

Evidentiary notice: Allegations are presented as allegations. Publication does not establish guilt, determine the truth of a claim or constitute a judicial finding. Each document retains its original evidentiary status.
